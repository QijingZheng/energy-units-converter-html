Energy Unit Converter

Type a value into any field and every other unit updates instantly. Constants are the exact SI definitions plus CODATA 2022.

nm and fs scale as 1/E and are defined only for E > 0; every other unit is proportional to the energy and accepts negative values (binding energies, energy differences). Both 1.5e-3 and Fortran-style 1.5d-3 are accepted.

4 · Conventions and caveats
  • Definition of fs: here fs is the oscillation period τ = 1/ν = h/E, so 1 eV ↔ 4.135668 fs, matching the original Python script. If you want a lifetime / decoherence time ħ/E (1 eV ↔ 0.6582120 fs), divide by 2π.
  • nm and fs are inversely proportional to the energy and undefined for E ≤ 0; those two fields are then left blank and marked «undefined».
  • kcal is the thermochemical calorie: 1 cal ≡ 4.184 J (exact), not the 15 °C or IT calorie.
  • J refers to a single particle; kJ/mol and kcal/mol are per mole, the two differing by NA.
  • K is the equivalent temperature E/kB, with no 3/2 or other degree-of-freedom factor.
  • Hartree is the atomic unit (a.u.) of energy, 1 Ha = 2 Ry.
  • Small differences from the original Python script: energy_unit_conv.py uses pre-2018 CODATA values (h = 4.13566733e-15 eV·s, e = 1.60217733e-19 C) whereas this page uses the exact 2019 SI definitions; the two start to differ in the 8th significant digit.
  • Works offline: the page needs no server-side code at all — save it and open it locally. Only the formula typesetting is enhanced by KaTeX when online; offline it falls back to plain-text formulas and the conversion itself is unaffected.
